What is deleted

Your Keeper account and associated personal profile, personal documents and metadata, personal chat history, saved personal memories and usage records will be deleted. Organization content shared with other members may first require ownership transfer or may be retained in anonymized form where necessary to preserve shared workspace records.

Timeline and retention

Verified requests are processed within 30 days. Limited records may be retained only when required for security, fraud prevention or legal compliance, as explained in the Privacy Policy.
